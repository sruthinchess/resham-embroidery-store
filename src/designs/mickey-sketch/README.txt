Notes
--------------------------------------------------------------
* Mickey Mouse is a Disney trademark. Fine to stitch for yourself;
* selling it would need a licence.
* Single colour line art. The white of the face and gloves is cut
* out, so stitch on a light fabric.
* The 4K images are reconstructed from the traced colour regions at 4K,
  so they show exactly what the stitch file contains. Detail is limited
  by the resolution of the source image.
