Notes
------------------------------------------------------------
* Thread colours are stored in PES, PEC, JEF, VP3, XXX, PCS, PCQ, PCD,
  PCM, SEW, JPX and TBF. The commercial formats (DST, EXP, DSB, DSZ, GT,
  TAP, STX, EMD, INB, NEW, ZXY, U01) carry the colour stops but not the
  colours - assign threads in the order listed above.
* Fill is a 45 degree tatami at 0.4 mm row spacing; each shape also has
  an edge-run underlay. The white linework is a separate running stitch
  layer - use white or a very light thread, or delete that layer if you
  prefer the veins left as bare fabric.
* Stitch the design on medium-weight woven fabric with a tear-away or
  cut-away stabiliser.
