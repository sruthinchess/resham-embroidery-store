Notes
--------------------------------------------------------------
* Thread colours are stored in PES, PEC, JEF, VP3, XXX, PCS, PCQ, PCD, PCM, SEW, JPX, TBF.
  The commercial formats (DST, EXP, DSB, DSZ, GT, TAP, STX, EMD, INB,
  NEW, ZXY, U01) keep the colour stops but not the colours - assign
  threads in the order listed above.
* Fill is a 45 degree tatami, 0.4 mm row spacing, with an edge-run
  underlay. Shapes narrower than about 0.85 mm are stitched as a
  running line down their centre instead of a fill.
* Stitch on medium-weight woven fabric with tear-away or cut-away
  stabiliser. Use a 75/11 sharp needle and 40wt rayon or polyester.
